Elementum subtitle adapter

Based on SubBridge, copyright (c) 2026 subzero75, MIT License.
Source: https://github.com/subzero75/kodi-subbridge
The complete license is retained in:
resources/lib/elementum_py/subtitle_bridge/LICENSE.txt

The adapted files retain SubBridge's native Kodi subtitle listing, public
Stremio subtitle protocol, IMDb identification, language mapping and ranking.
Changes: Elementum entry dispatch and settings, exact playback context and
release names, Hebrew encoding normalization, bounded network/archive/cache
handling, public-server URL validation and redacted diagnostic logging.

Protocol reference:
https://github.com/Stremio/stremio-addon-sdk/blob/master/docs/api/responses/subtitles.md
The default source is https://opensubtitles-v3.strem.io . Availability and
language coverage depend on that service. No account credentials are bundled.

Existing Wizdom/Ktuvit add-ons are separate providers and are not modified or
copied into this distribution. Kodi remains responsible for rendering and
subtitle timing controls; text is not reversed to simulate right-to-left.
